About

My career runs the length of the mobile network. I started on intelligent network and value-added services at Reliance Communications and Ericsson, built real-time billing software at Amdocs, automated IMS operations at Jio, and most recently led 5G core design and deployment at Rogers Communications in Vancouver.

I own work from design through production: writing the engineering specifications and test plans, automating the lifecycle with Ansible and Python, validating changes in the lab with what-if simulations, and watching the KPIs once they go live. I'm now completing a Master of Management (Software Projects) at the University of Victoria's Gustavson School of Business.
